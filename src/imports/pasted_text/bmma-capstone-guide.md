─── CHAPTER I – INTRODUCTION ──────────────
Sections (shown as checklist items with completion toggles):
  ☐ A. Project Context
       Establish background, relevance, and problem framing.
  ☐ B. Capstone Project Questions
       State 3–5 clear, researchable questions aligned with objectives.
  ☐ C. Objectives of the Project
       Must directly answer the capstone project questions.
  ☐ D. Scope, Limitations, and Delimitations
       Define boundaries and constraints of the project.
  ☐ E. Review of Related Literature
       Cite recent, relevant sources using APA 7th edition.
  ☐ F. Theoretical / Conceptual Framework
       Include a diagram with written explanation.

─── CHAPTER II – METHODOLOGY ──────────────
  ☐ A. Research Design and Approach
       Describe the design, rationale, and alignment with objectives.
  ☐ B. Participants and Data Gathering
       Identify sample, instruments, and data collection procedure.
  ☐ C. Ethical Considerations
       Address data privacy, informed consent, responsible media practice.
  ☐ D. Development and Evaluation Process
       Cover requirement analysis, design, production workflow, and testing.

─── CHAPTER III – RESULTS  ★ FINAL DEFENSE ─
Add a highlighted banner: "★ Emphasized during the Final Defense Presentation"
  ☐ A. Presentation of Data
       Use clear tables, graphs, and figures with proper labels.
  ☐ B. Pre- and Post-Implementation Findings
       Show measurable impact with comparison data.
  ☐ C. Project Implementation
       Document rollout, technical execution, and site/platform requirements.

─── CHAPTER IV – DISCUSSION & OUTPUT  ★ FINAL DEFENSE ─
Add a highlighted banner: "★ Emphasized during the Final Defense Presentation"
  ☐ A. Interpretation of Findings
       Explain results logically, supported by data.
  ☐ B. Answers to Capstone Project Questions
       Respond to each question clearly and completely.
  ☐ C. Conclusions and Recommendations
       State significance, practical improvements, future directions.
  ☐ D. Project Prototype / Final Multimedia Output
       Submit the finished creative output with documentation.
       Include evidence-based justification of all design decisions.

───────────────────────────────────────────
5. FINAL DEFENSE PREPARATION GUIDE
───────────────────────────────────────────
A visual step-by-step card:

Step 1 – Polish Chapters III & IV
  Focus your presentation on Results and Discussion/Output.
  Ensure pre/post-implementation data is complete and visualized.

Step 2 – Finalize Your Multimedia Output
  Your output must reflect research-based creative decisions.
  Prepare documentation justifying design choices.

Step 3 – Prepare Your Presentation
  You have 60 minutes to present. Practice your flow:
  [Intro – 10 min] [Ch. III – 20 min] [Ch. IV + Output – 25 min] [Wrap-up – 5 min]

Step 4 – Anticipate Panel Questions
  Panel Q&A = 30 minutes. Know your methodology, data, 
  and creative decisions inside out.

Step 5 – Dress Code & Logistics
  Wear corporate or business attire for the Final Defense.
  Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled slot.
  Bring 1 hard copy of the manuscript + soft copy of the output.
